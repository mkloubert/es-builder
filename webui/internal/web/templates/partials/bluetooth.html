How to put the controller in pairing mode
  • Xbox: Hold the pairing button on top of the controller until the Xbox logo blinks fast.
  • PlayStation: Hold PS + Share (DS4) or PS + Create (DualSense) until the light bar blinks.
  • 8BitDo: Hold Start + Y (Switch mode) or Start + X (X-Input), then press the Pair button for 3 s.

During scanning and pairing the device accepts pairing requests without a PIN.
